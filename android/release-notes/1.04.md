**Third fix for custom GPU drivers (Turnip).** The broken picture with a Turnip driver (black columns and stripes over the scene and the touch buttons) turned out to come from how Turnip draws, not from how the frame reaches the screen: the black columns are exactly as wide as the tiles Turnip draws in on the Adreno 740, and most of each tile was lost. With a custom driver installed, the game now tells Turnip to draw every pass directly into memory instead of in tiles (`TU_DEBUG=sysmem`).

The changes of 1.02 and 1.03 (waiting before each frame is shown, copying frames by the CPU) did not help and are taken out again, so HDR works with a custom driver again.

Drawing without tiles can cost some speed with Turnip. Nothing changes with the phone's own driver. If the picture is still wrong, please send a short recording and Log > Share, or go back to the phone's driver (Settings > GPU driver > Use the phone's driver).
