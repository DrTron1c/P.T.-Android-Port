The first public release of **P.T. Android Port**: P.T., the 2014 PS4 teaser by Kojima Productions, running natively on Android, built on the [P.T. PC port](https://github.com/LoreanXavier/pt-pc). Not an emulator. No game files are included: it runs from your own copy of the game.

**You need**
- Android 10 or newer, arm64, with Vulkan 1.3 (a recent Snapdragon, Mali or Immortalis chip on Android 13 or newer is the realistic floor)
- your own copy of P.T. (US release, CUSA01127): a dump folder or a fake PKG (a store PKG cannot be used)
- several GB of free space for the copy of the game files the app makes

**Install**
1. Download `P.T.PC.Port-Android-arm64.apk` below and open it (allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks).
2. Start the app and choose your dump folder or fake PKG; keep the screen on until the copy is done.
3. After that the app finds newer versions by itself when it starts, and the main menu has a **Check for updates** entry.

**What is in it**
- touch controls drawn from the P.T. HUD, and controller support
- a main menu made after the title screen: the flickering bulb, with its buzz, creaks, footsteps and low music
- a settings panel for phone and tablet GPUs: presets, picture size, frame rate limit, frame counter, lighting, textures, effects and an experimental built-in upscaler
- save manager with backups, a log screen and a report when the game closes unexpectedly

**Good to know**
- Tried on one device so far (Snapdragon 8 Gen 2). Reports from other devices are welcome: use Log > Share in the app and open an issue.
- The spoken-word recognition of the desktop game is not in the Android app: tap the JACK button where the game listens.
- Builds are signed with a key kept in this repository so that updates keep your game files; take APKs from this page only.
