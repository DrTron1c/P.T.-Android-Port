**Second fix for custom GPU drivers.** 1.02 did not cure the picture with a Turnip driver (black blocks and red stripes over the scene and the touch buttons, HDR on or off). The cause is how Turnip writes the system's display buffers on some phones: the screen reads them in another layout. With a custom driver installed, the game now no longer hands its frames to the system that way: it draws into its own images and copies each finished frame into the window itself.

With a custom driver: HDR is not used, and the copy costs a millisecond or two a frame. With the phone's own driver nothing changes.

If the picture is still wrong with your driver, please send a short recording and Log > Share, or go back to the phone's driver (Settings > GPU driver > Use the phone's driver).
